# Model sites, by mode

110 real sites, live and checked on 3 November 2026, chosen because their design is **drawn from their subject** rather than from a template. They complement the galleries in `resources.md`: where a gallery shows what is fashionable right now, this list says **exactly what to borrow** from each site.

## How to use it (step 5 of the process)

- Pick 3 sites in the project's mode **and at least 1 outside its sector**: a bicycle maker learns more from a tinned-food brand than from another bicycle maker.
- Borrow **one quality** per site (the one in the "Borrow" column, or another named just as precisely), never a page, never the identity (logo, signature palette, licensed typeface, illustrations).
- Read the "Avoid" column: many of these sites work thanks to material the project does not have (photos, budget, content).
- Open the site before drawing on it: sites change, and the description below may be out of date. If the site has changed or is no longer online, say so and pick another one.
- Record the URL and the borrowed quality in the report (step 5 and delivery).

## Limits of this list

- Verified by searching and reading the pages as text: each site's existence is confirmed, but some visual descriptions (colours, typefaces) come from the research agents' knowledge rather than from a screenshot. Treat them as to be confirmed when the site is opened.
- The Awwwards, Godly, SiteInspire and Commerce Cream galleries blocked automated reading; the evidence comes mainly from Minimal Gallery, One Page Love, Httpster, ecomm.design, siiimple and the sites themselves.
- Review every quarter alongside `tells.md`: a heavily copied site becomes a source of tells, and leaves the list.
